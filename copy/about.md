# About (live at intellectualreads.substack.com/about, set 2026-10-12)

Stored in the publication's `subscribe_content` field. Edit via `PUT /api/v1/publication`.

Intellectual Reads is about difficult books. Poetry. Romantic prose. The American classics.

I have logged 303 books since 2018. Twenty of them are McCarthy. Most were not easy, and the hard ones are the ones I still think about.

Every week, a review. One book, a line worth keeping, and the edition I would buy — usually a first edition or a Vintage International paperback, linked so you can find a copy.

And once a quarter, one hard book read together. Twelve weeks, about thirty pages a week, with notes on each section and a thread to argue in. The first is *Blood Meridian*, starting Monday, January 4.

The reviews are free. The group read is for paid subscribers — $8 a month or $80 a year, opening December 1. Until then you can pledge.
