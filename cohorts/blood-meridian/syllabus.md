# Blood Meridian: cohort 1 syllabus

Cormac McCarthy, *Blood Meridian, or the Evening Redness in the West* (1985).
23 chapters and an epilogue, about 351 pages. 12 weeks, Monday 2027-01-04 to Sunday 2027-03-28.

**How the weeks were cut.** Each chapter's length was measured in words from a local copy
of the text (`scripts/split_chapters.py`, output in `split.json`). The book runs 116,737
words over 351 pages, so 1 page ≈ 333 words. The 23 chapters were split into 12 runs of
whole chapters, keeping each week's load as even as the chapters allow. Most weeks
are 27–35 pages. Two outliers can't be avoided without splitting a chapter: chapter 13
(22 pp) stands alone, and chapters 14–15 (40 pp) are the heaviest week. Page counts are
approximate. They're derived from word counts, not from a printed page map.

**Saved passages.** quotes.json gives no location for his four Blood Meridian quotes.
Each one was placed by matching its opening words against the text
(`scripts/locate_quotes.py`). All four matched exactly one chapter.

**Discussion prompts are DRAFT.** Claude wrote them as starting points. Approve them,
rewrite them or cut them. **Chapter notes are his and are left empty.**
